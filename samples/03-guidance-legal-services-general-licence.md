## One-pager

# Guidance: Legal Services General Licence

## What happened
OFSI published guidance covering the Legal Services General Licence (INT/2026/11112108) and introduced a reporting form for legal fees.

## Who is affected
Firms that provide or receive legal services under UK sanctions, including banks and fintechs that engage legal counsel, must consider the licence and reporting requirements.

## 3 actions for compliance teams
1. Verify whether your organisation’s legal service activities fall under the new licence scope.
2. Obtain and complete the legal‑fees reporting form where applicable.
3. Update internal sanctions screening and monitoring controls to incorporate the licence requirements.
