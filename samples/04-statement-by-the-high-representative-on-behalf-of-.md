# Statement by the High Representative on behalf of the EU on the alignment of certain countries concerning restrictive measures against the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea

## What happened
The High Representative announced the alignment of certain third countries with Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2283, amending Decision (CFSP) 2016/849 concerning restrictive measures against the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea.

## Who is affected
Banks, fintechs and other regulated entities that apply EU sanctions screening for the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea and for transactions involving the newly aligned third countries.

## 3 actions for compliance teams
1. Verify that sanction screening tools include the latest alignment information for the identified third countries.
2. Review transaction monitoring rules to capture potential DPRK‑related activity involving entities from those jurisdictions.
3. Update internal policies and training to reflect the amended restrictive measures framework.
